01ABOUT

Two disciplines,
one drafting table

Engineering gives the structure. Design gives the language to explain it. I use both — sometimes on the same project.

I'm a final-year Mechanical Engineering student at Kalyani Government Engineering College (Batch 2027), based in Kolkata, West Bengal. My work sits between two rooms — the CAD lab, where I model gears, pistons and chassis, and the design desk, where I lay out posters, banners and thumbnails.

As Convenor of the Students' Automobile Club of KGEC, I helped a 23-member team design, fabricate and race a CV-category go-kart at the Indian Karting Race in Coimbatore. I've also trained on the factory floor — CNC machining and missile assembly at Ordnance Factory Dum Dum, and steel processing at SAIL's Alloy Steels Plant, Durgapur.

Outside the workshop, I run the camera for Shutterbug-KGEC and lead layout for Imposter-KGEC, the college's design club — where the same eye for tolerances and alignment carries over from drafting to design.
